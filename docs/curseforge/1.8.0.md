# Quest Targets 1.8.0

## Massive Improvements

- **One Master Button for your active targets.** The master button searches the quest log and your enabled RestedXP, Polestar, ItemTree and RareScanner sources together. Outside combat, each click puts nearby targets with known coordinates first, including NPCs ready for quest turn-ins.
- **Search all modules** is on by default under **Show master button**. Turn it off to search only quest log targets with the master button. The blue button searches RestedXP guide targets.
- **RareScanner** checks nearby readable nameplates first and updates the distance order on every click outside combat, including while standing still.

## Target markers

- Under **Target Markers**, choose **Raid marker**, **Ping**, or **No marking** for each targeting button. **Ping** is available in WoW Forever and Retail. WoW’s own settings control whether pings show in chat.

Thanks to **Caedis** for suggesting target pings!

## Options

- Addon settings are organized under **Quest Targets** and **Buttons & Bar**. Open **Patch Notes** and **Feedback** under **General**.
- Under **Appearance & Position**, enable **Show bar background** for a dark WoW background behind the button bar. The bronze border is slightly thicker and the dividers stay centered.

## Known Issues

- In the German WoW Forever client, some NPC names vary by gender. QuestieDB supplies one form per NPC, so exact-name searches can miss the variant shown in-game, such as **Diebin der Wüstenläufer**.

**Supported clients:** WoW Forever, Retail, Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic and Mists of Pandaria Classic.
