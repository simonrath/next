# Quest Targets 1.7.5

## Polestar on WoW Forever

- With Polestar installed, turn on **Follow Polestar guide targets** under **Buttons > Targeting bar**. Your master button searches NPCs from the displayed guide step, including alternate sources for collection goals. A valid friendly NPC stays selected when you click again.
- If the guide step has no target, the master button falls back to your quest objectives. If both guide integrations are active and available, RestedXP takes priority.

Thanks to **postblink** for the Polestar integration!
