# Next 1.8.4

### SilverDragon
- **New: SilverDragon support!** Use the purple Rare button or its hotkey to search for known rares in your current zone. Turn off **Scan all rares in zone** to target the rare focused on SilverDragon’s world map.
- The Rare button appears automatically with a compatible RareScanner or SilverDragon installation. RareScanner takes priority when both are available.

### Patch notes
- A dwarf and a murloc greet you in the in-game Patch Notes window.
- **Show new patch notes on startup** is off by default. You can turn it on at the bottom of the Patch Notes window.

### Default settings
- **Automatic module activation:** Next enables integrations and shows their targeting buttons when compatible addons load. Saved choices to turn modules off still apply.
- The quest window starts hidden. Module search, dividers, button tooltips and muting the target-clearing sound are on. Bar spacing starts at 0 px. The Rare button uses Ping on supported clients.
- On Forever, **Defaults** in Next’s options resets only Next settings.

Next supports Forever, Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary, Mists of Pandaria Classic and Retail. Addon integrations require compatible versions of the corresponding addons.
