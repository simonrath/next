# Quest Targets 1.7.4

## Quest list

- Quests are grouped alphabetically by the zones in your quest log. Click a zone heading out of combat to collapse or expand it. Your choices are saved per character.

## Targeting bar

- Lock the bar to prevent accidental movement while right-dragging. Enable **Lock button bar position** under **Buttons > Targeting bar**, or hold **Shift** over the bar out of combat and click the lock in its top-left corner.

Thanks to **virtual_pip** for suggesting this on CurseForge!

## Patch Notes and feedback

- Check **Don't show next Patch Notes** to skip automatic opening after updates. You can still read the notes and change this setting under **Options > AddOns > Quest Targets > Patch Notes**.
- In the feedback window, scan instructions now appear above the QR code and copy instructions appear directly above the URL.

One download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, WoW Forever and Retail.
